This matrix comes from 2 reference TikTok slideshows (7 slides received).
- **Deck A** (slides 3, 4, 5, 8): HTN 20% → MTN 30% → LTN 25% → PSL app card.
- **Deck B** (slides 2, 3, 5): HIGH TESTOSTERONE → AVERAGE TESTOSTERONE → PSL app card.

## 1. The pattern

### Slide anatomy
| Slot | Role | What's on it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook** | A question or label that makes the viewer place themselves ("Which one are you?") |
| 2 … N-1 | **Tier ladder, maximum → minimum** | Same AI face, one tier per slide. Big white label at the top, optional stat line under it ("20% of population") |
| N | **CTA / payoff** | PSL rating app card with the **top-tier** face: `PSL 6.2 • Chad` / `POTENTIAL 6.7 • Chad`, with the App Store icon |

### The face (the actual product)
- **One identity, locked.** It's the same AI man on every slide: same bone structure, ethnicity, eye colour, hair colour.
- **Passport framing.** Frontal shot, neutral or slightly intense expression, black crew-neck tee, flat grey seamless backdrop, even studio light.
- **Only the "downgrade levers" change**, a little more on each slide:
  - jaw and neck: sharp and thick → soft and thin
  - eyes: hooded, low-lidded "hunter" eyes → droopy, tired, rounder, with under-eye shadow
  - brow: low and heavy → higher and lighter
  - face fat: lean → puffy
  - skin: clear and tan → dull
  - hair: full and textured → limp, flat and messy
  - posture: chin-up and confident → slightly slumped
- **Steps are subtle on purpose.** HTN and MTN look almost the same, which gets comments like "bro they're the same guy" and "MTN mogs HTN". That's the comment engine.

### The story
It's a **status fall**: you watch the same guy lose rank. Viewers rank themselves against the ladder as they go, then the final slide hands them a tool to get their own number. The CTA scores the *top* tier at only 6.2 with a "potential" of 6.7, so even the best version has room to improve. That's the bridge to routines and the app.

### Two formats
| Format | Tiers | Label style | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Long ladder** | 5–6 | Community acronyms + "% of population" | Chad / HTN / MTN / LTN / Sub5 |
| **Short ladder** | 3 | Plain English: one variable at High / Average / Low | Testosterone |

## 2. Master template (fill-in)

| Slide | Text on slide | Face direction |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Hook | `[HOOK]` | Top-tier face, or a split of top vs bottom |
| 2 | `[TIER 1 / MAX]` + `[x% of population]` | Max: every lever at its best |
| 3 | `[TIER 2]` + `[x%]` | Very close to max; one lever slightly softer |
| 4 | `[TIER 3]` + `[x%]` | Two or three levers softer; eyes start to droop |
| 5 | `[TIER 4]` + `[x%]` | Jaw clearly softer, puffier, tired eyes, flatter hair |
| 6 | `[TIER 5 / MIN]` + `[x%]` | Every lever at its worst, but still clearly the same guy |
| 7 CTA | PSL app card: `PSL [score] • [tier]` / `POTENTIAL [higher score] • [tier]` | Tier 1 face, often chin-up |

Rules:
- Make the percentages add up to about 100%. Make the middle tier the biggest so most viewers land there.
- Never jump identity. The downgrade has to read as "same guy, worse version".
- The CTA always scores the top tier and shows Potential > PSL.

## 4. Hook bank (slide 1)

- "Which one are you? Be honest"
- "The [X] scale explained"
- "Where do you rank?"
- "Same guy. [N] different [variable]."
- "This is why you're not a [top tier] yet"
- "Most guys are [middle tier] and don't know it"
- "[Variable] tier list"
- "Rate yourself, then check the last slide"

## 5. Caption and comment bait

- Caption: "comment your tier 👇" / "be honest which one" / "the app on the last slide is crazy"
- The near-identical top two tiers start "they're the same" arguments. Keep that subtlety.
- The % stats start "no way it's 20%" debates. Keep them plausible but arguable.
- The CTA score (top tier at only 6.2) starts "how is that only a 6" replies, and points people to the app.

## 6. Production checklist

- [ ] One base face generated, and every tier edited from it (not regenerated)
- [ ] Same tee, backdrop, lighting and framing on every slide
- [ ] Each tier changes 1–2 levers more than the one before; the bottom is still recognisable
- [ ] Bold white sans-serif label at the top, stat line under it (long-ladder format)
- [ ] Percentages add up to about 100%; the middle tier is the biggest
- [ ] CTA card: top-tier face, PSL score < Potential score, App Store icon
